About / How to play

Escape the Website is a five-chapter storybook escape game set inside a hidden house within an old website. Travel through the Bedroom, Library, Dark Room, Workshop, and Final Door to collect missing diary pages and find your way back outside.

How to Play

  • Tap or click objects around each room to inspect clues and collect items.
  • Select an item from your bag—or drag it—and tap a spot to use or combine it.
  • Need a nudge? Tap Hint to summon the firefly guide, and your progress saves automatically.
